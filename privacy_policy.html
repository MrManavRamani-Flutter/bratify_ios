1. Introduction & Core Privacy Philosophy

Welcome to Bratify ("we," "our," or "the App"). We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and protecting your personal data. We believe privacy is a fundamental human right.

🛡️ 100% On-Device Processing: All meme creation, 500+ frame styling, photo cropping, color grading, and text compositing execute strictly on your local iPhone or iPad.

🚫 Zero Cloud Uploads: We operate zero remote servers, zero cloud databases, and zero tracking beacons.

✨ Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, store, sell, or share any of your personal information or media.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike traditional photo editing apps, Bratify operates completely offline and without cloud reliance. We strictly DO NOT collect:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect your name, email, phone number, address, Apple ID, or billing details.
  • No User Accounts or Logins: No registration, login, profile creation, or password management is required.
  • No Image or Media Telemetry: Photos selected from your Photo Library or Camera remain strictly in local RAM and device sandbox. They are never transmitted externally.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not track IDFA, IDFV, MAC addresses, or IP addresses.
  • No Location Data: We do not request, access, or store GPS or geolocation coordinates.
  • No Biometric Data: The App does not perform facial recognition, facial geometry scans, or biometric identification.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

Under Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1, Bratify requests explicit, purpose-driven iOS permissions:

A. Photo Library Access (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription & NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription)

Purpose: Allows you to select photos to place into multi-photo frames (1 to 4 slots), collage grids, and custom background wallpapers, as well as save high-resolution rendered creations directly into your Apple Photos camera roll.

Scope: Photo access is temporary, isolated, and processed locally via Flutter Canvas. Your photos never leave your device.

B. Camera Access (NSCameraUsageDescription)

Purpose: Allows you to take a live photo directly within the App to place into an aesthetic frame or canvas background.

Scope: The camera feed is displayed exclusively on your physical screen and is never transmitted or recorded remotely.

4. Local Storage & Draft Persistence

Bratify includes an on-device Saved Library & Drafts system:

  • SQLite Sandbox: All saved designs, slot photo references, custom background parameters, typography styling, and filter presets are saved locally inside an isolated SQLite database on your device.
  • User Ownership: You retain complete ownership. You can delete drafts anytime within the app, and uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local data.

5. Third-Party Services, SDKs & Advertising

  • Zero Advertising Networks: Bratify contains NO third-party ad networks (no Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, etc.). You will never see third-party ads.
  • Zero Tracking SDKs: We do not integrate external user analytics or tracking beacons (no Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, AppsFlyer, Mixpanel, etc.).
  • Apple Native StoreKit: In-app rating links navigate directly to Apple's native App Store using official Apple HTTPS protocols.

6. Children's Privacy Protection (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Protecting children's privacy is paramount. Bratify does not target, solicit, or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). Because our App collects zero personal information from any user, it is fully compliant with COPPA and global child privacy standards.

7. Global Regulations Compliance (GDPR, CCPA / CPRA)

Because Bratify does not collect or process personal data on external servers:

  • GDPR (EEA): Your data rights (Access, Erasure, Portability) are fully respected as your data remains in your physical possession on your device.
  • CCPA / CPRA (California): We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
