## 1. Introduction & Core Privacy Philosophy

Welcome to **Bratify** ("we," "our," or "the App"). We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and protecting your personal data. We believe privacy is a fundamental human right.

**Bratify is built on a 100% On-Device, Privacy-First Architecture.** This means:
* We **do not collect, store, sell, or share** any of your personal information.
* All meme creation, 500+ frame styling, photo cropping, color grading, and text compositing take place **entirely locally on your iPhone or iPad**.
* We operate **zero remote servers**, **zero cloud databases**, and **zero user tracking beacons**.

By using Bratify, you enjoy full creative freedom with complete peace of mind that your photos, text, and personal creations never leave your personal device.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike traditional photo editing and social utility applications, Bratify operates completely offline and without cloud reliance. We strictly **DO NOT collect**:

1. **No Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, Apple ID, or billing details.
2. **No User Accounts or Logins:** There is no registration, profile creation, password management, or social login integration required to use any feature of the App.
3. **No Image or Media Telemetry:** Any photos you select from your Photo Library or capture with your Camera remain strictly in your device's local memory (RAM) and local sandbox storage. They are **never** transmitted to us or any third party.
4. **No Device Identifiers for Tracking:** We do not collect, read, or track Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), Identifier for Vendors (IDFV), MAC addresses, or IP addresses.
5. **No Location Data:** We do not request, access, or store GPS or geolocation coordinates.
6. **No Biometric Data:** The App does not perform facial recognition, facial scanning, or biometric identification.

## 3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

To provide creative photo editing, frame styling, and export capabilities, Bratify requests explicit, purpose-driven permissions from iOS/iPadOS under Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1:

### A. Photo Library Access (`NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription` & `NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription`)
* **Purpose:** 
  * Allows you to select photos from your device to place into multi-photo frames (1 to 4 slots), collage grids, and custom background wallpapers.
  * Allows you to save your rendered high-definition creations (memes, posters, and collages) directly to your Apple Photos camera roll.
* **Scope & Processing:** Photo access is temporary and localized. Photos are rendered directly onto Flutter's local canvas engine. Your photos are never uploaded, monitored, indexed, or shared externally.

### B. Camera Access (`NSCameraUsageDescription`)
* **Purpose:** Allows you to take a live photo directly from within the App to immediately place into an aesthetic frame or canvas background.
* **Scope & Processing:** The camera feed is displayed exclusively on your physical screen. No live camera feed or captured snapshots are streamed or sent to any remote server.

You can grant, modify, or revoke these permissions at any time via your device settings:  
**Settings ➔ Privacy & Security ➔ Photos / Camera ➔ Bratify**.

## 4. Local Storage & Draft Persistence

Bratify includes a local **Saved Library & Drafts** feature that allows you to save and re-edit your creations at any time:

* **SQLite Local Sandbox:** All saved designs, slot photo references, custom background parameters, typography styling, and filter presets are saved locally inside an isolated SQLite database on your device.
* **Data Ownership:** You retain 100% ownership and control over your saved creations.
* **Deletion:** You can delete any saved draft directly within the App at any time. Furthermore, uninstalling the App completely removes all local app data from your device.

## 5. Third-Party Services, SDKs & Advertising

* **Zero Advertising Networks:** Bratify contains **NO third-party advertising SDKs** (such as Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, AppLovin, etc.). You will never see third-party ads or trackers in Bratify.

## 7. Global Privacy Regulations Compliance

### A. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — European Economic Area (EEA)
Under the GDPR, individuals have rights regarding their personal data, including the right of access, rectification, portability, and erasure. Because Bratify does not collect, process, or store any personal data on external servers, your data is always in your complete physical custody on your personal Apple device.

### B. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)
Under the CCPA and CPRA, California residents have the right to know what personal data is collected and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information:

* We **do not share** your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
* We do not collect sensitive personal information.
