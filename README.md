### 🔍 iPhone Screen-by-Screen Detailed Analysis:

* **Screen 01 (Studio Hub & Templates):**
  * **Branding & Visuals:** Deep carbon studio backdrop (`#121212`) contrasted with authentic Brat Lime (`#8ACE00`).
  * **120+ Viral Quotes Engine:** Horizontally scrollable quote chips categorized into 10 themes (*Brat Summer, Pop Culture, Relatable, Sassy, Minimalist, etc.*). Tapping any quote instantly opens the editor with text synchronized to canvas layers.
  * **Curated Template Cards:** High-density 2-column grid showcasing Polaroid, Digicam ISO, Cyber Y2K, Music Players, and Collage starters.
  * **Pre-Flight Badges:** Clear visual indicators displaying required photo counts and aspect ratios (1:1, 4:5, 9:16).
  * **Floating Glass Dock:** Frosted glass bottom bar with haptic feedback connecting Studio, Editor, Saved Library, and Settings.

* **Screen 02 (Real-Time Canvas Editor):**
  * **Live Interactive Canvas:** 60fps real-time compositing rendering crisp text against the signature lime backdrop.
  * **Draggable Multi-Text Overlays:** Free touch drag-and-drop text positioning with normalized relative coordinate persistence.
  * **Typography Controls:** 30+ Google fonts, font size slider, font weight pills, and authentic low-res text blur slider.
  * **Dual Background Engine:** 1-tap toggle between solid colors/gradients and custom photo backdrops with opacity and blur controls.
  * **Layer Manager & History:** Dedicated bottom sheet to manage, duplicate, or reorder text layers, backed by full Undo/Redo.

* **Screen 03 (Multi-Photo Collages & Frame Customizer):**
  * **Dynamic Multi-Slot Layouts:** Full native support for 1 to 4 photos (`single`, `split2H`, `split2V`, `polaroidDuo`, `triptych3`, `filmstrip3`, `grid4`).
  * **Dedicated Slot Toolbar:** Interactive pills (`Slot 1`, `Slot 2`, etc.) allowing creators to pick, crop, or clear individual slots independently.
  * **Non-Destructive Frame Switching:** Seamlessly switch between collage layouts without losing loaded images or custom typography.
  * **Granular Border Customization:** Real-time sliders for border thickness, custom border colors, corner radius, and slot spacing.

* **Screen 04 (Dedicated Full-Screen Crop & Adjust Studio):**
  * **Distraction-Free Workspace:** Full-screen modal workspace isolated from canvas clutter for maximum precision.
  * **Fluid Multi-Touch Gestures:** Smooth pinch-to-zoom and drag-to-pan positioning to frame photos accurately.
  * **Precision Transform Controls:** 90° clockwise rotation button, fine `-45°` to `+45°` tilt angle slider, and horizontal/vertical mirror tools.
  * **Rule-of-Thirds Grid:** 3x3 alignment guide overlay for balanced composition.
  * **1-Tap Social Presets:** Instant aspect ratio switching: `1:1 Square`, `4:5 Portrait`, `9:16 Story/Reel`, `16:9 Landscape`, and `3:4 Classic`.

* **Screen 05 (Saved Drafts Library & Lossless Re-Editing):**
  * **SQLite On-Device Archive:** Responsive grid displaying all user creations with instant reactive updates upon saving.
  * **Zero-Loss 1-Tap Re-Editing:** Reopening any draft restores every slot image, background wallpaper, text layer position, filter, and transform.
  * **Ultra-HD Direct Export:** Renders high-fidelity 3x Retina PNG files directly to Apple Photos with zero compression and zero watermarks.
  * **iPad Popover Crash-Safe Sharing:** Contextually anchored sharing sheet ensuring smooth sharing on iPhones and iPads.

## 🌟 Complete App Features & Capabilities

### 🟢 1. Iconic Brat Meme Studio & 120+ Quotes Engine
* **Signature Aesthetic:** Authentic lime-green background (`#8ACE00`), low-res blur styling, and high-contrast typography.
* **Instant Palette Switching:** Seamlessly toggle between Classic Brat Lime, Crisp Minimalist White, Dark Carbon Studio (`#121212`), or fine-tune custom colors with the RGB palette picker.
* **120+ Viral Quotes Engine:** 10 curated categories (*Brat Summer, Pop Culture, Relatable, Sassy, Minimalist, Nightlife, Self-Care, Irony, Energy, Party*).
* **1-Tap Creative Sync:** Selecting any quote automatically opens the studio editor and syncs canvas text overlays and frame captions in real time.

---

### 🖼️ 2. 500+ Aesthetic Frames & Multi-Photo Collages (1–4 Photos)
* **Dynamic Multi-Photo Layouts:** Single photo, Split 2H, Split 2V, Polaroid Duo, Triptych 3, Filmstrip 3, and 2x2 Grid 4.
* **Per-Slot Photo Control Toolbar:** Dedicated slot selector pills (`Slot 1`, `Slot 2`, etc.), allowing independent photo replacement, clear slot, and per-slot crop adjustments.
* **Non-Destructive Switching:** Switch frame layouts without losing loaded slot images or custom text styling.
* **Granular Frame Customization:** Adjustable border width, customizable border color, corner radius sliders, and inner slot spacing.

---

### 🌄 3. Dual Background Engine (Solid Color vs. Custom Wallpaper)
* **Dual Background Modes:** Toggle seamlessly between **Solid Color / Gradient** and **Custom Canvas Background Image**.
* **Custom Wallpaper Import:** Bring in custom photos, textured backdrops, or aesthetic wallpapers directly from your Photo Library or Camera.
* **Background Opacity Slider:** Fine-tune backdrop transparency from `0%` to `100%` for subtle watermarks or high-impact contrast.
* **Background Gaussian Blur:** Smooth slider to create creamy bokeh backdrops behind photo frames and text layers.
* **Flexible BoxFit Options:** Switch effortlessly between `Cover`, `Contain`, and `Fill` display modes.
* **1-Tap Reset:** Instantly revert back to signature Brat Lime at any time with a single tap.

---

### 🔤 4. Unconstrained Typography & Multi-Text Drag Engine
* **Free Drag-and-Drop Positioning:** Place text anywhere on the canvas with smooth gesture drag-and-drop and normalized relative coordinate persistence.
* **Multi-Text Layers Manager:** Add unlimited text layers, edit captions, duplicate layers, reorder, or delete layers in a dedicated bottom sheet.
* **30+ Curated Google Fonts:** Bold display fonts, retro monospace, clean sans-serifs, handwritten scripts, and editorial serifs.
* **Full Typography Controls:** Font size slider, font weight, letter spacing, line height, text blur slider, text alignment, casing, colors, and drop shadows.
